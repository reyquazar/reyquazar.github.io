Privacy Policy for Color My Days

Last updated: October 11, 2026

This privacy policy applies to the Color My Days Android application (package name: com.colormydays.app).

Data Collection

This app does not collect, store, or share any personal information. No analytics, advertising, tracking, or crash reporting services are used.

Local Data Storage

All data you create in the app (color tags, day colors, notes, settings) is stored locally on your device only using Android DataStore. This data is never sent to any server, third party, or the developer.

Data Export and Import

The app allows you to export your data as a JSON file and import it back. This file is created and saved by you, on your own device or storage. The developer has no access to it.

Permissions

The app does not request any sensitive permissions. It does not access your camera, microphone, location, contacts, or files outside of the export feature you explicitly trigger.

Children's Privacy

The app does not knowingly collect any data from children under 13. Since no data is collected at all, the app is safe for all ages.

Third-Party Services

The app does not integrate any third-party services that collect data, including but not limited to: Google Analytics, Firebase, Facebook SDK, or advertising networks.

Data Deletion

If you uninstall the app, all your data is permanently deleted from your device. You can also delete all data from within the app using the "Reset all days" option in Settings.

Changes to This Policy

This policy may be updated from time to time. Any changes will be reflected on this page with a new "Last updated" date.
